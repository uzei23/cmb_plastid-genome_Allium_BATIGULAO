## Plastome Summary

The *Allium sativum* chloroplast genome is a complete circular plastid genome with a total length of **153,172 bp**. The genome has the typical quadripartite organization of a plant plastome, consisting of a **Large Single-Copy (LSC) region, two Inverted Repeat (IR) regions, and a Small Single-Copy (SSC) region**.

The reported region sizes are:

* **LSC:** 82,035 bp

* **SSC:** 18,015 bp

* **IRa:** 26,561 bp

* **IRb:** 26,561 bp

The genome contains protein-coding genes, tRNA genes, rRNA genes, and pseudogenes. Important functional gene groups include photosynthesis-related genes such as *psa* and *psb*, ATP synthase genes (*atp*), cytochrome b6f genes (*pet*), *rbcL*, RNA polymerase genes (*rpo*), ribosomal protein genes (*rpl* and *rps*), and other conserved plastid genes.

## Gene Content and Important Observations

The annotated GenBank record:


* **Total gene features:** 135

* **Protein-coding/CDS features:** 83

* **tRNA genes:** 38

* **rRNA genes:** 8

* **Pseudogene features:** 6

The plastome contains genes involved in photosynthesis, ATP synthesis, electron transport, transcription, translation, and other plastid functions. Examples include *psaA*, *psbA*, *atpB*, *petA*, *rbcL*, *rpoB*, *rpl2*, and *matK*.

The genome also contains genes located within the inverted-repeat regions, resulting in duplicated gene copies. Pseudogene features are also present in the annotation. Introns are present in several annotated genes.

The Galaxy analysis showed an overall **GC content of 36.68%**. The genome consisted of a single sequence record of **153,172 bp** and contained **zero gaps or ambiguous N bases**.
